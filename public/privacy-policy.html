Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 13, 2026

Bhaktamar Stotra is designed to work offline. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or with third parties.

What the app stores

The app stores your selected language and font-size preference locally on your device using on-device storage. These preferences stay on your device and are not sent to a server.

Analytics, ads, and accounts

The app does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, account creation, cloud sync, or background tracking.

Permissions

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or other sensitive runtime permissions for its current functionality.

Data retention and deletion

Because the app only keeps local preferences on your device, you can remove that data by clearing the app storage or uninstalling the app.
